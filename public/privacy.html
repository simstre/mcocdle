Privacy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

MCOCdle is a free fan-made guessing game for Marvel Contest of Champions. There are no accounts or sign-ups, and we never ask for your email address or any other contact detail.

What stays on your device. Your guesses, progress, display name and a random player ID are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing this site's data deletes them.

What the leaderboard stores. When you solve the daily puzzle, the display name you chose, your guess count, the time you solved it and your random player ID are sent to our server so you can appear on that day's leaderboard. This record is public to other players and is deleted automatically after 48 hours. Pick a nickname rather than your real name if you prefer.

Who receives data. The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes requests (including IP addresses) to deliver pages and stores the leaderboard. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches them. There are no analytics, ads, cookies or tracking, and nothing is sold.

MCOCdle is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with Kabam or Marvel.
